### 3. DETAILED METHODOLOGICAL SPECIFICATIONS

#### 3.1 Dimension A: Market Structure
- **Causal Swings (`swing_high`, `swing_low`):**
  - *Mathematical Formula:* $P_i$ is a peak if $H_i = \max(H_{i-5 \dots i+5})$.
  - *Causality Rule:* Confirmed and emitted at $t = i + 5$. At bar $t$, the confirmed swing high is the peak from bar $t - 5$. Future lookahead is strictly zero.
  - *Known Limitation:* In choppy, volatile markets with narrow ranges, swing levels may be closely spaced, generating frequent false reclaims.
- **Fair Value Gaps (`bullish_fvg`, `bearish_fvg`):**
  - *Formula:* Bullish if $L_t > H_{t-2}$; Bearish if $H_t < L_{t-2}$. Magnitude normalized by $\text{ATR}_{14}$.
  - *Known Limitation:* In high-volatility regimes, FVGs are formed frequently but can be rapidly closed without providing support.

#### 3.2 Dimension B: Momentum & Trend
- **Wilder's ADX (`adx_14`):**
  - *Formula:* $DX = 100 \times \frac{|+DI - -DI|}{+DI + -DI}$; $ADX = \text{WilderEMA}(DX, 14)$.
  - *Known Limitation:* Lagging indicator; requires $2 \times 14 = 28$ bars of warm-up before stabilization.
- **Trend Persistence (`trend_persistence`):**
  - *Formula:* Number of consecutive closed 5m bars where $Close > EMA_{21}$ (positive) or $Close < EMA_{21}$ (negative).
  - *Known Limitation:* Extreme persistence values ($> 40$ bars) often indicate exhaustion / climax regimes rather than safe continuation entries.

#### 3.3 Dimension C: Volatility & Market Regime
- **Kaufman Efficiency Ratio (`efficiency_ratio_20`):**
  - *Formula:* $KER = \frac{|C_t - C_{t-20}|}{\sum_{i=1}^{20} |C_i - C_{i-1}|}$.
  - *Interpretation:* $KER \in [0, 1]$. Values $> 0.40$ indicate clean directional trends; values $< 0.25$ indicate noisy random walk.
- **4-State Regime Classification (`market_regime`):**
  - `HV_TREND`: $ADX \ge 22.0$, $KER \ge 0.32$, $\text{ATR Percentile} \ge 50\%$.
  - `LV_TREND`: $ADX \ge 22.0$, $KER \ge 0.32$, $\text{ATR Percentile} < 50\%$.
  - `HV_RANGE`: Trend criteria not met, $\text{ATR Percentile} \ge 50\%$.
  - `LV_RANGE`: Trend criteria not met, $\text{ATR Percentile} < 50\%$.

#### 3.4 Dimension D: Volume Proxies
- **Effort vs Result Anomaly (`effort_vs_result_ratio`):**
  - *Formula:* $\frac{\text{True Range} / \text{ATR}_{14}}{\text{RVOL}_{20}}$.
  - *Interpretation:* Ratios $\ll 1.0$ indicate high volume absorbed without price progress (climax/absorption). Ratios $\gg 1.0$ indicate thin liquidity vacuums.
  - *Governance Caution:* OHLCV volume does NOT reveal actual bid/ask book depth or aggressive vs passive flow.

#### 3.5 Dimension E: Cross-Asset Context
- **Market Breadth (`market_breadth_bull_pct`):**
  - *Formula:* $\frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^K \mathbb{I}(Close_{k, t} > EMA_{50, k, t})$ across all 10 canonical symbols.
  - *Interpretation:* Synchronized measurement of market-wide participation. Breadth $> 0.70$ signals broad bullish tailwinds; breadth $< 0.30$ signals systemic risk-off.
