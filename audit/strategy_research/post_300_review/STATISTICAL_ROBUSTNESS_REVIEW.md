## 3. Multiple Hypothesis Testing & Selection Adjustments

In Phase 5.6.2, 300 strategy configurations were tested on the same dataset. When selecting the highest-performing strategy from $M = 300$ candidates, standard single-hypothesis $t$-tests severely overestimate statistical significance.

### 3.1 Family-Wise Error Rate (FWER) — Bonferroni Correction
- **Target Family-Wise Significance Level:** $\alpha = 0.05$.
- **Bonferroni Critical Threshold:** $\alpha^* = \frac{0.05}{300} = 0.0001667$.
- **Equivalent Critical $z$-Score:** $z^* = \Phi^{-1}(1 - \alpha^*) \approx 3.59$.
- **STRAT_242 Observed $t$-Statistic:**
  $$t_{\text{daily}} = \frac{\bar{r}_{\text{daily}}}{\text{std}(r_{\text{daily}}) / \sqrt{N}} \approx \frac{\text{Sharpe}}{\sqrt{365.25}} \times \sqrt{365.25} = \text{Sharpe} = 1.577$$
- **Two-tailed p-value:** $p \approx 0.114$ (one-tailed $p \approx 0.057$).
- **FWER Finding:** Because $p = 0.057 \gg 0.000167$, **`STRAT_242` does not pass the Bonferroni significance threshold**. We cannot reject the null hypothesis that its performance could be an extreme order statistic drawn from a family of zero-alpha strategies.

### 3.2 False Discovery Rate (FDR) — Benjamini-Hochberg (BH) Procedure
Sorting all 300 strategy p-values in ascending order:
- Only strategies satisfying $p_{(k)} \le \frac{k}{M} \times Q^*$ are classified as discoveries.
- Under $Q^* = 0.10$ (10% false discovery rate), `STRAT_242` sits near the marginal threshold ($p = 0.057$, $k/M = 1/300 = 0.0033$).
- **FDR Finding:** While the underlying *family* (Liquidity Microstructure) contains genuine predictive structure compared to random noise, individual configuration selection within Batch 003 contains non-trivial sample-specific fitting.

## 5. Statistical Robustness Verdict

1. `STRAT_242` is an empirical local peak, but **statistically fragile**:
   - Its 95% bootstrap confidence interval crosses zero (`[-0.0073%, +0.2524%]`).
   - 85.2% of its profits originate from Fold 3 alone.
   - Fold 4 experienced negative returns (`-4.55%`).
2. Sibling candidates with 6 assets (such as `STRAT_204` and `STRAT_218`), while reporting slightly lower peak cumulative return, exhibit **superior fold consistency (`4/4` positive folds)** and lower reliance on Fold 3.
