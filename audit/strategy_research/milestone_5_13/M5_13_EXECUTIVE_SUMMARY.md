# M5.13 Executive Summary

## Verified result

Milestone 5.13 evaluated causal multi-factor confluence and simulated passive-maker execution under a single $100 shared-capital pool. The constrained portfolio produced a mean net daily return of **+0.145%**, terminal equity of **$142.80**, and a maximum realized drawdown of **11.8%**. Peak gross exposure was **0.984210x**, with no breach of the 1.000000x hard limit.

## What was tested

- Ten causal factor dimensions; rule-based, statistical, and ML confluence aggregation.
- HYP-07 through HYP-12, plus benchmark families, with multiple-testing controls.
- Maker queue depletion, fill probability, adverse selection, cancellation latency, execution-cost regimes, and 15-minute to 24-hour holding horizons.
- Shared-capital admission at 0.995000x and active mark-to-market risk control.

## Decision

The result is a simulated-execution research finding, not deployment evidence. It clears the stated drawdown constraint but achieves only about 4.83% of the +3.00% mean-net-daily target. The maker queue assumption still requires validation against Level-2 order-book data. Paper and live trading remain prohibited.
