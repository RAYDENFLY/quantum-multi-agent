## 1. Core Operating Principle: Autonomous 24/7 Execution

QUANTUM is conceived and engineered to operate continuously (24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year) as a **fully autonomous algorithmic trading system**, operating under strict human governance oversight.

### Autonomous Decision Authority
The system is explicitly designed to make and execute real-time trading decisions without human-in-the-loop latency or manual trade confirmations. **The Human Supervisor is NOT required to review or approve individual trades.**

Manual confirmation is strictly excluded from all routine trading operations, including:
1. **Asset Selection:** Dynamic scanning and candidate selection across the formally approved asset universe.
2. **Directional Decisions:** Directional signal classification (Long vs Short) generated by frozen quantitative models.
3. **Execution Decisions:** Precise timing of order placement, entry execution at market open (`open[t+1]`), and limit/market fill execution.
4. **Order & Barrier Lifecycle Management:** Dynamic or static take-profit (TP) and stop-loss (SL) barrier placement, cancellation, or adjustment in accordance with approved policy.
5. **Position Sizing:** Calculating trade notional in strict accordance with approved volatility budgets and equity limits.
6. **Routine Order Management:** Intrabar monitoring, time-decay barrier expirations (12-bar horizon), and cancellation of stale order intents.

## 2. Division of Responsibilities: Human Supervisor vs Autonomous System

The QUANTUM governance architecture enforces a strict division of authority between macro policy governance (Human) and micro operational execution (System):

```text
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    OPERATIONAL AUTHORITY DIVISION                                       |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|   HUMAN SUPERVISOR (MACRO GOVERNANCE)         |   QUANTUM SYSTEM (AUTONOMOUS MICRO EXECUTION)           |
+-----------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------+
| * Strategy & mathematical methodology approval | * 24/7 continuous market and orderbook data monitoring  |
| * Portfolio-level risk limits & budget floors | * Dynamic specialist signal ingestion & scoring         |
| * Maximum aggregate exposure caps (Gross / Net)| * Autonomous Long / Short directional decisions         |
| * Asset universe boundaries & whitelist policy | * Automated entry, fill, and barrier execution         |
| * Capital allocation & reserve policies       | * Position sizing strictly within approved limits       |
| * Master system activation & deactivation     | * Bar-by-bar position tracking and lifecycle management|
| * Emergency manual halt / intervention kill   | * Automatic, non-bypassable risk constraint enforcement |
| * Formal Phase 5.6 production authorization   | * Real-time event logging, telemetry, and health alerts|
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### A. Human Supervisor Responsibilities
The Human Supervisor acts as the policy director, risk boundary setter, and emergency circuit-breaker:
- **Strategy & Methodology Approval:** Formal evaluation and sign-off on model architectures, barrier formulations, feature pipelines, and accounting methodologies (e.g. Gates `DEC_01` to `DEC_06`).
- **Portfolio Risk Policies:** Establishing binding risk parameters, including maximum portfolio drawdown tolerances, per-trade stop-loss risk budgets, and daily loss thresholds.
- **Exposure & Concurrency Caps:** Authorizing limits on aggregate gross notional exposure ($\sum |N_i| \le \text{Cap} \times \text{Equity}$) and maximum simultaneous active specialists.
- **Capital Allocation Policy:** Authorizing initial equity, reserve maintenance margins, and deleveraging rules.
- **System Activation & Deactivation:** Initiating or standing down autonomous agent loops.
- **Emergency Intervention:** Exercising emergency kill-switches to halt trading or liquidate positions during black-swan events, exchange insolvency, or critical system anomalies.
- **Production Authorization:** Providing formal, uncompromised human authorization before any model or code can be deployed to live production capital (Phase 5.6).

### B. Autonomous System Responsibilities
The QUANTUM autonomous engine executes trades within the human-authorized boundaries:
- **Continuous Market Monitoring:** Real-time ingestion and validation of WebSocket orderbook and candle feeds across whitelisted assets.
- **Asset Selection & Signal Generation:** Scanning asset universes, computing technical and ML features, and generating calibrated directional predictions.
- **Directional & Barrier Execution:** Placing entry orders at next candle open, anchoring exit barriers, and executing fills causally without discretionary delay.
- **Independent Risk Enforcement:** Checking proposed trades against the 12-gate risk engine ([`src/quantum/agents/risk.py`](file:///d:/quantum-multi-agent/src/quantum/agents/risk.py)) and rejecting any order that breaches constraints, regardless of ML confidence.
- **Lifecycle FSM Tracking:** Maintaining order states via deterministic finite state machines ([`src/quantum/agents/banker.py`](file:///d:/quantum-multi-agent/src/quantum/agents/banker.py)) from `PENDING_OPEN` through `OPEN` to `CLOSED`.
- **Telemetry & Health Monitoring:** Generating immutable audit logs of every evaluation, signal, order, fill, rejection, and MTM equity change.

## 3. Mandatory Safety Design Requirements

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The following items are architectural and operational **safety design requirements** for live automated deployment. They are explicitly documented as target specifications and must not be misconstrued as claims of already-certified live production implementation.

1. **Independent, Non-Bypassable Risk Enforcement:**  
   Risk limits must be enforced deterministically at the architectural gatekeeper level ([`RiskAgent`](file:///d:/quantum-multi-agent/src/quantum/agents/risk.py) and exchange gateway). Large Language Models (LLMs) or neural predictive models must never have the authority to bypass, modify, or override risk constraints.
2. **Crash Recovery & Idempotent Restart:**  
   System restarts or process crashes must be completely idempotent. On reboot, the engine must query exchange state, reconcile open orders and positions against its local ledger, and resume monitoring without creating duplicate orders or duplicate positions.
3. **Deterministic Emergency Stop Protocol:**  
   Emergency stop mechanisms must explicitly define position handling:
   - *Soft Stop (Freeze Entries):* Halts new signal generation and trade entries while allowing active positions to exit normally via established TP/SL barriers.
   - *Hard Stop (Emergency Liquidation):* Cancels all open orders and routes market liquidation orders for all active positions to exchange matching engines.
4. **Exchange Disconnection Safe-State Policy:**  
   Loss of exchange WebSocket or REST API connectivity must trigger an automated safe state: cancel unconfirmed pending orders, suspend new trade entries, and, if disconnection persists beyond a predefined threshold (e.g. 60 seconds), verify via backup channels or trigger local defensive contingency protocols.
5. **Immutable Auditability & Event Traceability:**  
   Every trade proposal, risk gate verdict, order submission, fill execution, slippage deviation, funding cash flow, and barrier exit must be recorded in an append-only audit trail with UTC microsecond timestamps.
6. **Hard Exposure & Leverage Ceilings:**  
   The system must be mathematically incapable of silently exceeding approved gross exposure caps ($\sum |N_i|$) or per-asset position limits, scaling back order sizes pro-rata or vetoing excess entries before submission to the exchange.

To maintain absolute institutional clarity, four distinct governance events are strictly separated:

```text

|                                  FOUR SEPARATE GOVERNANCE DOMAINS                                       |

                                            v
  [Domain 3] Phase 5.5 Research Execution Authorization (Gate 4 Authorization)

             Status: NOT_AUTHORIZED (Strictly isolated from Phase 5.5 outcomes)
```

- **No Manual Trade Confirmation:** Designing or requiring human approval for individual routine trades is explicitly rejected as inconsistent with the QUANTUM operating model.
- **No Conflation of Research & Production:** Authorizing research backtests (Gate 4) does NOT authorize live trading. Live trading (Phase 5.6) requires an independent operational review, infrastructure qualification, exchange key security audit, and formal human sign-off.
